**Usage** held in 1 hand; **Bulk** L

Bottled lightning is packed with volatile reagents that create a blast of electricity when they're exposed to air. Bottled lightning deals the listed electricity damage and [electricity splash damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=29). On a hit, the target becomes [off-guard](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=58) until the start of your next turn. Many types grant an item bonus to attack rolls.
