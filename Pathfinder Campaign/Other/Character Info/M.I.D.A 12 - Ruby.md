#### The Nightmare
MIDA finds themselves upon the very battlefield where they once awoke. Around them, what had once seemed like an ordinary field is now revealed as a land ravaged by war. Above, the sky burns a fiery red as thunder cracks and lightning strikes across the battlefield. Before them, people run in terror — not with the joy and celebration of a festival, but away from MIDA, as though they are the true danger.
As if no longer in control of themselves, MIDA slowly raises their right hand and snaps their fingers. Instantly, the nearest person, a helpless elven woman, bursts into flames, collapsing to the ground as she screams in agony.
