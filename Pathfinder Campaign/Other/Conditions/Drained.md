Your health and vitality have been depleted as you've lost blood, life force, or some other essence. **Drained** always includes a value.

You take a status penalty equal to your drained value on Constitution-based rolls and DCs, such as Fortitude saves.

Your maximum Hit Points are reduced by an amount equal to your **level × your drained value** (minimum 1 per point of drained).
Whenever your drained value **increases**, you also lose a number of Hit Points equal to your **level × the amount your drained value increased**. Losing these Hit Points doesn't count as taking damage.
For example, if you're a 3rd-level character and become **Drained 3**, you lose 9 Hit Points and reduce your maximum Hit Points by 9.
If you're already **Drained 2** and become **Drained 3**, you lose only 3 additional Hit Points, and your maximum Hit Points are reduced by an additional 3, for a total reduction of 9.
