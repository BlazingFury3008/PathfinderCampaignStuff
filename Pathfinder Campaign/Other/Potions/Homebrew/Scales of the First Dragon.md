**Source**: Homebrew  

Some would call this more of a magical potion than an alchemical creation. Whatever its true nature, it has the power to instill either unwavering confidence or crippling doubt in those who come into contact with it.
When you throw this bomb, it explodes in a **5-foot burst**. Each creature in the area is affected by either **Honor** or **Enmity**, as chosen when the item is created. The effects of **Honor** or **Enmity** are determined by the item's level.

**Activate: 1A**

## Lesser (Item 2)
**Price** 30 gp  
**Bulk** L  
**Honor**: The creature gains 1d4 to attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks, its duration is **1 round**
**Emnity - DC 16:**  The creature takes a 1d4 penalty to attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks, its duration is **1 round**
## Moderate (Item 6)
**Price** 80 gp  
**Bulk** L  
**Honor**: The creature gains 2d4 to attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks, its duration is **1 minute**
**Emnity - DC 20:** The creature takes a 2d4 penalty to attack rolls, saving throws and ability checks, its duration is **1 minute**
## Greater (Item 12)
