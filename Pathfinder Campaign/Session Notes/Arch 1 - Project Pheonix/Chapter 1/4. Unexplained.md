## Recap
After finding themselves trapped in the tunnels, the party discussed the current events, until the three tunnels opened up. When given the choice between the three tunnels; [[The Mountains#Underground|The Volcanic Underpass]], [[Ancient Ruins#Tunnels Behind Victor's|The Unexplored]], and [[The Laboratory]], The party chose [[Ancient Ruins#Tunnels Behind Victor's|The Unexplored]]

![[Ancient Ruins#Tunnels Behind Victor's|The Unexplored]]

As they explored the room, [[M.I.D.A 12 - Ruby]] felt themselves pulled towards the throne, and once they touched the throne, they were thrown into vision of their past 

![[M.I.D.A 12 - Ruby#The Nightmare]]

Whilst they experienced this event of PTSD, [[Ash - Elliot]] and [[Urist - Adam]] explored the cavern, noticing runic markings on the throne and around a book on the floor. Some simple arcane knowledge gives [[Urist - Adam]] and [[Ash - Elliot]] that the runes are Binding and/or calling to something. 

[[Urist - Adam]] makes a copy of the runes as [[Ash - Elliot]] looks into the journal which appears to have some form of encryption.

Once the party finished exploring, they went back to the fork of tunnels, and exited through the middle tunnel, finding themselves exiting at [[Cael Varen]]'s forge.

[[Urist - Adam]] waited behind and had a conversation with [[Cael Varen]], asking about [[Black Bronze]], and their fire-arms. Finding out that there were no others in the city that could forge black bronze

[[Ash - Elliot]] experimented with [[M.I.D.A 12 - Ruby]], given them a concoction, coming to the realisation that [[M.I.D.A 12 - Ruby]] was made up of some living metal. After this discovery, the party decided to head for the night, with [[M.I.D.A 12 - Ruby]] and [[Urist - Adam]] staying in the [[Dragonclaw Tavern]] and [[Ash - Elliot]] in their accomodation

Before shutting off for the night, [[M.I.D.A 12 - Ruby]] noticed a shadowy creature resting on the chest of [[Urist - Adam]]
