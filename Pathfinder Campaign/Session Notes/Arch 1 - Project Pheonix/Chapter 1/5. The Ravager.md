## Recap
After escaping the ancient tunnels beneath Eldermount, the party began piecing together the growing mystery surrounding Project Phoenix.

Ash spent the morning deciphering Dr. Frederick Gransley's journal, uncovering the horrifying truth behind his experiments. What began with willing volunteers slowly descended into kidnapping innocent people before Gransley ultimately turned his work upon himself. The final pages spoke of strange shadowy figures, his writings becoming increasingly incoherent as madness consumed him.

Meanwhile, Urist and M.I.D.A. visited Cael Varen, questioning him about Black Bronze, his recent work, and Ruby's unusual construction. Although Cael provided answers, little connected him directly to the growing conspiracy.

Instead of travelling to the Obsidian Vault immediately, Ash visited the excavation site beneath Eldermount, where the newly uncovered runes were identified as both Necril and Chthonian. Shortly after, the first violent earthquake struck the city.

Reunited once more, the party experienced another tremor before finally arriving at the Obsidian Vault to speak with Magister Selera Vayne.

From House Malakai's records, the party learned that faint traces of Abyssal magic had been discovered beneath the marketplace, leading investigators towards the Cinderways before the tunnel collapsed. Previous victims all bore mysterious "FG" markings, while reports from another city described an entire village being destroyed before the creature responsible was somehow killed by a perfectly circular hole through its head.

The investigation also revealed growing suspicions surrounding Project Phoenix, with several prominent figures—including Cael Varen, Uriel Stormwind, Marra, Jorick, and Byron—being documented for their skills, connections, or unusual circumstances, though no definitive evidence had yet been uncovered.

As the discussion came to an end, another violent impact shook the Vault.

Magister Selera Vayne's expression changed instantly.

> "**Apprentices, prepare the defenses... we're under attack.**"

And then...

A massive fist burst through the walls of the Obsidian Vault.

### Stage 3

With The Ravager defeated, the monstrous body begins to collapse. The black ooze slowly peels away, revealing [[Victor]] lying motionless beneath the mass. The courtyard falls silent as the earthquakes cease, and for a brief moment it appears that the battle is over.

End initiative.

Allow the players time to breathe, investigate the scene, heal, or speak amongst themselves. [[Magister Selera Vayne]], [[Cael Varen]] and [[Uriel Stormwind]] lower their guard, believing Victor to have died during the transformation.
Victor is **Feigning Death**.
He is conscious, listening to everything around him, waiting for someone to lower their guard and approach.
Unless a player succeeds on a **DC 20 Medicine** or **DC 20 Perception** check, Victor appears completely dead. His body is covered in burns, scars and surgical incisions from Dr. Frederick Gransley's experiments.
The moment a creature willingly moves within **5 feet** of Victor, his eyes snap open.

Combat immediately resumes.

---

Victor no longer possesses the overwhelming strength of The Ravager, but his mind has been completely shattered. He lashes out with bombs, syringes and scalpels, speaking to unseen figures throughout the encounter.

Throughout the fight, Victor should mutter fragmented sentences such as:

> "No... no... the dosage was wrong..."
> "Can you hear them...?"
> "Don't let him see you..."
> "He's watching..."
> "It wasn't supposed to hurt..."

The players should quickly realise they are no longer fighting a monster, but a man whose mind has been completely destroyed.

---

Should the party attempt to spare Victor rather than kill him, [[Magister Selera Vayne]] will intervene once the fighting has ended.
Victor's body can be healed with ease.
His mind cannot.
Experimentation, prolonged exposure to unstable alchemical compounds and the influence of an unknown force have left him psychologically broken. Recovering would require months of deprogramming, detoxification and constant magical supervision, with no guarantee of success.
Even if treatment were successful, Victor would never truly be the man he once was.
Should the party instead choose to end his suffering, Victor dies without ever regaining a moment of clarity, still speaking to voices only he can hear.
