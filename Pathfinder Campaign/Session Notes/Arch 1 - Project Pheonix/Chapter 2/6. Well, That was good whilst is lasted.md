## Recap
Given the events of Chapter 1, the party will have gotten 3 week (21 days) of downtime. Once all the downtime has been dealt with, no-matter where the three are in the city, they will each mystically recieve a letter

```
**To the Defenders of Eldermount**
Word has reached us from the northern settlement of Sanctuary
Three nights ago, the settlement suffered an assault by creatures matching the descriptions given following the incident in during the festival.
We request your presence at the Chantry immediately

- Selera Vayne
```

This should encourage the party to go to [[The Obsidian Vault]], where Selera will be awaiting them, [[Corin Vale]] and [[Cael Varen]] will be awaiting them, [[Cael Varen]] having made some modifications to [[The Ashen Blade]], allowing for faster travel, and [[Magister Selera Vayne]] calling upon the bandit to provide transportation to the city of Sanctuary
