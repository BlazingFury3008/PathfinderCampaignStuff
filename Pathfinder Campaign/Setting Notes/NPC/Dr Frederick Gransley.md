## Background
[[Dr Frederick Gransley]] was once a human prodigy within the [[Eldermount's Alchemist Guild]], they were both a prodigal Alchemist and Engineer; Creating the blueprints of many of the technologies used within the city of [[Eldermount]]. 
However, after some time, they began to be haunted by visions, creating alchemical formulas that many of the guild saw to be abominations. After a failed test, creating the first [[Infected]], [[Dr Frederick Gransley]] Was never seen again,

However, [[Dr Frederick Gransley]] fled to the caverns beneath the city, utilizing alchemical concoctions to seal off the entrance to the cave. with fear of starving to death, [[Dr Frederick Gransley]] attempted to create a concoction that would allow them to live forever, instead they turned themselves into a [[Fleshwarp]].
